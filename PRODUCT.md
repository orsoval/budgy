# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Individuals seeking a straightforward, stress-free way to monitor personal finances. They need to track expenses, manage savings goals, and monitor recurring bills without cognitive overload or financial stress.

## Product Purpose
A personal finance budgeting application that simplifies transaction entry, categorizes spending, tracks progress toward savings goals, and visualizes financial trends. Success is defined by making the user feel confident and in control of their financial data with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality
Clean, reliable, and approachable. The tone is calm, trustworthy, and clear, avoiding flashy gimmicks or dense financial jargon.

## Anti-references
- Overly complex dashboard templates with cluttered grids and unnecessary data points.
- SaaS design clichés such as heavy purple-to-blue gradients, decorative glassmorphism, or nested cards.
- Low-contrast gray text on tinted backgrounds that hinders readability.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity Over Clutter**: Present key information (balance, main charts, recent transactions) with plenty of breathing room. Hide advanced actions until requested.
- **Data Precision**: Ensure numerical values use clear, legible typography (e.g., tabular numbers) and proper alignment to make financial data instantly scannable.
- **Calm Interaction**: Animations and transitions should be functional and subtle, avoiding noisy or distracting decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target WCAG AA conformance (minimum 4.5:1 contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text).
- Support for system-wide accessibility preferences, including reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion`) for any charts or modal transitions.
